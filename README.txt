iStore Attingal — Premium black & gold website concept by Nexshaly Studio

PREVIEW
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a browser.

GITHUB PAGES
Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and the entire assets folder to the ROOT of a new public repository.
Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.

IMPORTANT
- Keep the supplied shop sign image at assets/istore-sign.png. This path fixes the broken image shown in the earlier deployment.
- Confirm business details, phone, hours, Google rating and WhatsApp with the owner before launch.
- Product tiles are illustrative placeholders, not confirmed stock or prices.
- Obtain the shop's Instagram profile and permission before embedding videos.
- This is an unofficial concept, not the shop's live website.
- No admin dashboard, inventory system, online payment or offer manager is included.
